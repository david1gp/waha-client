## [0.5.0] - 2026-09-21

### 🚀 Features

- *(groups)* Normalize group jid and name responses

### ⚠️ Breaking changes

HTTP `GroupInfo` responses from `groupList`, `groupGet`, `groupCreate`, and `groupJoinInfoGet` now expose group identity and name as `jid` and `name`. The former response fields `id` and `subject` are removed.
